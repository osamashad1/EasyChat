Features

Built for the way small teams actually work

Every feature earns its place. If it adds noise, it doesn't ship.

Channels and threads

Organise talk by project or client. Reply in a thread to keep the main channel tidy.

  • Public and private channels
  • Threads with their own notifications
  • Pin key messages to the top

Direct and group messages

Quick one-to-one questions or small group chats, without creating a channel.

  • Up to 8 people per group DM
  • Online, away and focus status
  • Read receipts you can turn off

File sharing

Drop in images, PDFs and design files. Previews appear right in the conversation.

  • Drag and drop uploads
  • Inline previews for images and PDFs
  • 5 GB to 1 TB storage by plan

One-click calls

Start a voice or video call from any channel. Share your screen when words aren't enough.

  • Up to 25 people per call
  • Screen sharing
  • No extra app or plugin

Search that finds it

Find any message, file or link by keyword, person or date.

  • Filters for person, channel and date
  • Search inside PDFs
  • Full history on paid plans

Calm notifications

Focus mode, quiet hours and smart batching mean fewer pings and more work.

  • Quiet hours per person
  • Notify only for mentions
  • Daily digest of what you missed

Security and privacy

Encryption, two-factor sign-in and data hosted in the EU or US.

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Two-factor authentication
  • Export or delete your data any time

Works everywhere

Web, desktop and mobile apps that stay in sync.

  • iOS and Android apps
  • Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Offline drafts that send when you reconnect
