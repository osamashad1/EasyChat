Questions

Frequently asked

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Up to 10 people can use EasyChat free for as long as you like. You only pay if you want unlimited history, more storage or more people.

Can I change plans later?

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes, registered nonprofits and schools get Pro free for up to 50 people. Contact us with proof of status.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export everything in one click. We delete your workspace 30 days after cancellation, or sooner if you ask.
